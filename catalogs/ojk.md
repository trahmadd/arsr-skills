# OJK catalog - Sustainability Report content (SEOJK 16/SEOJK.04/2021 Lampiran II, Bagian III)

Source: `rujukan_regulasi_gri.md` section 1.2, taken from the full text of Lampiran II (third-party copy, truncated at Bagian IV). Cross-check against the official OJK PDF before printing. Lampiran II of POJK 51/2017 is shorter (A.1-A.7); SEOJK 16/2021 expands it to A-G and is the numbering used in reports.

Applicability notes:
- F.3 uses financing/investment wording. Non-financial companies mark it not applicable with a reason on the G.4 page.
- B.2 and F.9-F.16 are mandatory for businesses directly related to the environment; others cover at least energy and water.
- Economic and social summaries compare the last 3 years when the company has operated more than 3 years.
